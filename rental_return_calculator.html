1031 exit proceeds
1031 advantage: All capital gains & depreciation recapture are deferred. Your full net proceeds roll into the next property. At your income level this is the most powerful tax lever available — the deferred tax acts as an interest-free loan from the IRS into your next deal.
Rent modeled as fixed monthly amount stepped up by the specified dollar every 2 years. 1031 exchange defers 100% of capital gains and depreciation recapture. AGI >$150K: depreciation-driven passive losses suspended annually, carried into next property's basis on 1031. Mortgage amortization derived from balance, rate, and P&I.
